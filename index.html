Sentence Completion: Proactive Solving

Core Technique

Don't just jump directly into the options! Proactive Solving means predicting the tone and meaning of the blank before glancing at the answers.

1. Identifying Clues

Scan for context indicators inside the sentence that dictate the direction of the missing word.

2. Signposts (The Direction Markers)

🟢 Support Signs: and, similarly, in addition, since, also, thus, because, etc. (The thought continues in the same direction)
🔴 Contrast Signs: but, despite, yet, however, unless, rather, although, etc. (The thought reverses direction 180 degrees)

3. Root Word Clues

Affix Type Positive (+) Negative (-)
Prefixes pro, bene, philo non, un, im, in, mal
Suffixes ble, ant, ent, en less, cide

4. Sentence Structure Clues

  • Cause & Effect: accordingly, consequently, because, thus, for, since
  • Definition / Restatement: comma (,), semicolon (;), or colon (:)
  • Part of Speech: determining whether the missing slot demands a noun, verb, adjective, or adverb.
Kapil's Pro Tip: The 5-Second Blank Scan Mentorship By Kapil • FACE Prep

"Whenever you see a blank flanked by a semicolon (;) or colon (:), remember that the second half explains or mirrors the first half! If you see 'despite' or 'although', immediately invert the polarity. If clause 1 is positive, clause 2 MUST be negative."

Para-Jumbles: 7 Master Strategies

Rearrangement Blueprint

Para-jumbles test your ability to reconstruct logical discourse. Look out for the two primary varieties tested in campus recruitment:

Moving Jumbles: Sentences are free-floating with no fixed or anchor sentences. (e.g. A, B, C, D)
Anchor Jumbles: 1 or 2 anchor sentences are anchored at the beginning or end of the paragraph.
1
Find the Opening Sentence: Look for a standalone independent sentence introducing the core subject, person, or theme. Avoid opening with pronouns (he, she, they, this).
2
Identify Logical Pairs to Eliminate Options: Pair mandatory two-sentence combinations (e.g. cause and its immediate effect, question and answer). Then eliminate any choice lacking that pair.
3
Take Cues from Signpost Words: Connectors like 'however', 'furthermore', 'as a result', 'meanwhile' establish chronological and logical progression.
4
Match Pronouns with Antecedents: A proper noun always precedes its pronoun (e.g. 'Sachin Tendulkar' must come before 'he').
5
Look for Action Sequences: Reconstruct physical or temporal sequences (e.g. criminal nabbed → admitted to trial → awarded capital punishment).
6
Seek Generic to Specific Transitions: Broad global trends or general categories precede specific cases, individuals, or exceptions.
7
Clues about Abbreviations & Name Expansion: Full names or expanded acronyms (e.g. 'United States Navy (USN)') come before abbreviated references (e.g. 'the Navy' or 'USN').
Kapil's Elimination Formula: FACE Prep Hack

"In 90% of TCS and Infosys Para-jumbles, finding just ONE mandatory pair (like B-C) allows you to strike off 3 out of 4 options in under 15 seconds! Never arrange all 4 from scratch."
